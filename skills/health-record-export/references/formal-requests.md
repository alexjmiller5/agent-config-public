# Formal electronic self-copy requests

A patient portal's immediate visit summary can omit retained institutional
records. A formal request asks the institution's records team for a defined
copy, usually fulfilled later. It is a one-time request, not ongoing account
access or an automatic feed. Future refreshes need new requests for new or
changed records when the immediate export remains incomplete.

1. Use the institution's official patient records route and verify the entity,
   patient, recipient, requested dates and categories. Inspect current forms
   and terms. Reuse the user's actual scope and submission approval when it
   covers this request; otherwise obtain it before submission.
2. Request the actual missing content explicitly: full chart and scanned notes,
   hospital medication administration records and dose-level flowsheets,
   diagnostic images in original DICOM with all series and metadata, portal
   messages/attachments, and billing only when approved. Billing and imaging
   may be handled by separate departments. “All records” is not proof every
   category or every independent institution is included.
3. Prefer secure electronic delivery to the patient. Honor cost and consent
   boundaries: stop at a fee, financial obligation, signature or broader
   permission requiring a separate user decision. A photo-ID upload or identity
   attestation needs its own authorization when not already covered. Do not
   impersonate the user's signature or quietly accept new terms.
4. Record the institution, route, scope, dates, submission time, confirmation
   ID, delivery method, status/expiry and unresolved blockers in the private
   export register. Check pending requests before submitting again. Arm a
   supported notification wake and follow up through the approved route.
5. Download fulfilled originals, hash them, reconcile source IDs/content with
   existing records, and round-trip verify the destination. Compare each
   requested category against what arrived. A request acknowledgment or a ZIP
   labeled complete is not verified full coverage; retain omissions and
   separate follow-up requests as open gaps.
